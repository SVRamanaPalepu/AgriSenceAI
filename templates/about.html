About OptiCrop

OptiCrop (AgroSense AI) is an intelligent Crop Recommendation System designed to assist farmers, students, and agronomists in selecting the best-suited crops for their land with AI-powered explanations, field insights, and multi-model evaluation.

🌱 Soil Nutrients

Evaluates Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), and Potassium (K) levels and soil pH.

🌦 Climate Conditions

Considers Temperature, Humidity, and Rainfall for optimal crop growth.

🤖 AI Assistant

Natural-language conversational companion explaining recommendations and agronomic science.

📜 Prediction History

Maintains an organized log of previous field predictions with full details.
